# Loop-filter retune: R2 = 1.7 kOhm -> 3.6 kOhm (2026-10-11)

**Retuned loop filter: R2 = 3.6 kOhm. Original design: R2 = 1.7 kOhm.** C1 = 68 pF, C2 = 6.8 pF, Icp = 20 uA, N = 8, f_ref = 28.275 MHz, and everything else, unchanged.

## Why
With the corrected formulas (`wn^2 = Icp*Kvco_rad/(2*pi*N*C1)`, `zeta = R2*C1*wn/2`) and measured K_VCO = 887 MHz/V, R2 = 1.7 k gives zeta = 0.33 (PM ~30 deg, underdamped). R2 = 3.6 k gives zeta ~0.70 (PM ~65-70 deg by the 100*zeta rule; C2 ignored in the rule) at the same fn ~0.91 MHz. See `docs/loop_model.md`.

## How the results were produced
- Runs were generated by `sed`-substituting `r2=` (and the `.lib` corner) in `blocks/top/tb_closed_loop.spice` (coarse 0.85 V start) and `blocks/top/tb_closed_loop_coldstart.spice` (true 0 V start, 5x boosted pump), then running `ngspice -b` from the `pll-sky130` root. The substituted netlists and logs are next to the data (`*.cir`, `*.cir.log`, `*.txt`).
- Analysed with `scripts/analyze_closed_loop.py`; the 5 us ss runs (`ss5us_*`) were analysed with numpy.
- Lock = V_ctrl enters and stays within +/-2 % of its final value (spec < 2 us).
- Data: `results/loop/retune/` (tt; coarse and cold start; R2 = 1.7k / 3.0k / 3.6k) and `results/loop/retune_corners/` (ss/ff/sf/fs; R2 = 1.7k and 3.6k; ss also at 5 us).
- Only R2 = 1.7k / 3.0k / 3.6k were simulated at transistor level (plus an averaged-model sweep over 1.7-6 k with `scripts/loop_check.py`). R2 above 3.6k was not simulated; 3.6k is not claimed optimal.

## Results, tt 27 C, coarse 0.85 V start (nominal charge pump)
| R2 | lock | peak Vctrl | final f_vco | ripple (last 0.5 us) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.7k (original) | 577 ns | 0.930 V | 227.96 MHz (0.78 % high) | 8.6 mV p-p |
| 3.0k | 538 ns | - | 226.33 MHz | - |
| 3.6k (retuned) | 503 ns | 0.922 V | 226.18 MHz (0.008 % low) | 0.35 mV p-p |

## Results, tt 27 C, true 0 V cold start (5x boosted pump)
| R2 | lock | peak Vctrl | final f_vco | ripple |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.7k (original) | 839 ns | 1.008 V | 226.42 MHz | 2.6 mV |
| 3.0k | 1318 ns | - | 225.88 MHz | - |
| 3.6k (retuned) | 1352 ns | 1.001 V | 225.98 MHz (0.1 % low) | 0.35 mV |

## Results, five corners, coarse 0.85 V start, nominal charge pump
| corner | R2 = 3.6k lock | final f_vco (MHz) | R2 = 1.7k (original) |
|---|---|---|---|
| tt | 503 ns | 226.18 | 577 ns, 227.96 MHz |
| ff | 241 ns | 226.17 | not settled by 2 us: 225.17 MHz (0.45 % low) |
| sf (fast-NMOS / slow-PMOS) | 419 ns | 226.39 | not settled by 2 us: 229.13 MHz (1.3 % high) |
| fs (slow-NMOS / fast-PMOS) | 576 ns | 226.21 | not settled by 2 us: 228.90 MHz (1.2 % high) |
| ss | 967 ns (5 us run; settles to Vctrl = 0.9677 V, 0.1 mV ripple) | 2 us run: 224.56 (still settling) | 1737 ns in the 5 us run (3.5 mV ripple); at 2 us it was 3.6 % low (218.2 MHz) |

The original loop had not finished settling within the original 2 us run at ff/sf/fs/ss, so its lock times are not quoted there except ss (5 us run). The retuned loop locks inside 2 us at all five corners, slowest at ss (ss needs Vctrl ~0.968 V; weakest NMOS). Corner runs used the coarse start and plain charge pump; the 0 V cold start was simulated at tt only.

## Trade-off
Better damping, ripple and settling accuracy, faster coarse-start lock at tt (577 -> 503 ns) and at every corner inside 2 us; but the 0 V cold start is slower, 839 -> 1352 ns (+61 %). Both are inside the 2 us spec.

## Lock-metric sensitivity
The 503 ns at R2 = 3.6k is set by a small overshoot to 0.922 V sitting just outside the +/-2 % band (0.918 V) and decaying into it. The averaged model (`scripts/loop_check.py`, C2 included) overshoots slightly less, to 0.914 V, and enters the band at ~110 ns. The models agree on dynamics; the band metric is sensitive at its edge. (At R2 = 1.7k the averaged model gave 573 ns vs 577 ns at transistor level.)

## What does not change
All open-loop results (tempcos, spans, compensation fits, held-out checks, Monte Carlo, K_VCO values, label corrections): they use the free-running VCO with an ideal control voltage.
